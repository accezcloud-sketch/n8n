- Reservations: each stay with its timeline, payment and accepted policies; add ons sold with stays (meals, cleaning, transport)

- Communication: email templates (confirmations, check ins, cancellations), SMS messaging, and one inbox for conversations

- Owners and payouts: statements and payouts per owner
- Staff and roles (access control), and work orders (maintenance from request to done)

2. Service provider solution (page /solutions/service-providers/), for barbers, salons, spas, coaches and any appointment business:
- A marketplace listing where customers find and book them
- Bookings (accept, start, complete, no show), a calendar, and customer records (visits, spend, last visit)
